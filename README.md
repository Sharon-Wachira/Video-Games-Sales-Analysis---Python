# Video-Games-Sales-Analysis---Python
Analysed a gamers dataset to understand what genres generated most revenue, which regions has the highest sales and offered recommendations tol increase sales in regions performing poorly. 


## Business Problem

A gaming company wants to understand:
1. Which genres generate the most revenue.
2. Which regions drive the most sales.
3. Which publishers dominate the gamers industry.
4. How the gaming industry has evolved over time.


## Data Overview
 The gamers dataset contains 16598 games and  11 variables. The time period of the dataset is between 1986 to 2016. 

##### Key variables

They include:
•	Genre
•	Publisher
•	Regional Sales 
•	Global Sales
•	Platform
•	Year


## Business Questions

Which genres generate the highest global revenue?
Which regions contribute the most to game sales?
Which publishers dominate the gaming market?
How has gaming revenue changed over time?
Are blockbuster games driving industry revenue?
